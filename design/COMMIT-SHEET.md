# Commit sheet: Rommel Cabanal

**Intake.** One public portfolio for people hiring or commissioning a graphic designer or art director. Preserve the 22 projects, existing imagery, credits and contact channels. The public site is one HTML page; build a static replacement without the legacy jQuery carousel. Use Rommel’s existing work, not generated work or borrowed reference images. No deployment requested.

## 1. Signature
Keep the approved “Design you can hold.” hero: a still image of the Capilano University acceptance kit paired with a statement that the artwork immediately proves.

## 2. Color
Pure black `oklch(0 0 0)` is the continuous gallery ground, explicitly requested by the user. Restrained tonal palette: neutral white text, gray secondary text and hairlines, with charcoal reserved for hover and expanded rows. The artwork supplies all chroma. Overall full-page screenshot target mean L **0.22**, distinct from the black surface itself.

## 3. Type
DM Sans at regular to medium weights, including the hero. Newsreader stays a contrasting editorial accent in “hold.” and selected project titles. No new font family or default Inter; scale and placement replace heavy weights.

## 4. Grid break
The off-center hook cuts to Kapwa’s top-aligned 3:2 image pair, then the Convocation print with its facing note. Use one responsive gutter with no overlap or stagger. Preserve the grouped index.

## 5. Motion budget
One scroll family: short entrance treatments on the Kapwa packaging and Convocation print. Native scroll, no pinning, snapping, soundtrack, or initially hidden content. Reduced motion keeps both pictures still.

## 6. Reflex check
- First-order: a black portfolio usually defaults to enormous personal branding, glow, a marquee, and interchangeable project covers.
- Second-order: unstyled brutalist directory, tiny mono labels and no editorial hierarchy.
- Deviation: retain the original black but shoot the opening like a gallery display. Real project color, precise type hierarchy and a quiet archive do the work. The first cut drew its matte framing from [Babled’s monograph](https://babled.net/products/la-main-des-autres-monograph-book/); the surrounding palette now follows the [original portfolio](https://rommelcabanal.com/), not the light-and-green print references.

## 7. House tells broken
- Near-black stays by explicit brief; the absence of tint, glow and alternating dark bands keeps it tied to the original site rather than a default “premium” palette.
- Name as giant wordmark → a statement about the work, immediately evidenced by a real artifact.
- Tiny mono service labels → short title-case captions in the text face; no status bar or scroll instruction.
