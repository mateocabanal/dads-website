Brand identity

William Switzer

Project details

The William Switzer refresh preserves the furniture maker’s recognizable script signature while simplifying how it appears across print and web. Large serif typography and generous space give furniture photography a clear presence, with colour panels introducing emphasis where a layout needs it.

The catalog pairs styled images with product grids to support both browsing and reference. Advertisements focus the same editorial language on individual pieces, while website layouts make room for product views and studio imagery. Across all three, the furniture leads and the identity provides a consistent frame.
