Brand identity

Corporate Source Printers

Project details

This rebrand takes the grid familiar to a commercial and digital printer and makes it visible in the identity. Boxed lettering and a red-and-white palette give Corporate Source Printers a wordmark that can anchor a single card or repeat across a stationery system.

The grid determines how information is aligned and spaced across business cards, letterhead and envelopes. These printed pieces are a direct expression of the client’s business, so proportion, contrast and placement carry the identity with few supporting elements.
