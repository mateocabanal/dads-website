Capilano University Viewguide

Project details

The Viewguide gives prospective students a compact introduction to Capilano University. Its folded format stages the content: a simple illustrated cover sets the tone, an initial opening brings student photography and key statistics into view, and the full spread makes room for program information.

The folds are part of the editorial structure. Each panel needs to make sense at its own stage of opening and contribute to the complete layout. Aligning the visual hierarchy with the way the piece is handled makes it useful for a quick introduction and for later reference.
