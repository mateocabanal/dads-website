Capilano University International Student Guide

Project Description

A visually engaging, multi-panel publication designed to support Capilano University's international student recruitment efforts and provide recruitment representatives around the world with a comprehensive, easy-to-navigate resource.

Developed for the Centre for International Experience (CIE), the guide brings together academic programs, admission information, student experiences, and campus life into a cohesive printed piece. Combining expressive typography, vibrant photography, and structured editorial layouts, the publication balances the practical needs of prospective students with the university's welcoming and creative identity.

The creative approach centred on transforming a content-heavy recruitment guide into an engaging, accessible, and memorable experience.

A bold typographic cover introduces an energetic visual language, using layered messaging, vibrant colour gradients, and dimensional effects to capture attention. This expressive treatment contrasts with the more structured interior, where clear information hierarchy, modular layouts, and carefully organized program tables allow readers to navigate complex academic content with ease.

Authentic student photography and testimonials help establish a personal connection, while imagery highlighting the campus and its surrounding environment communicates the distinctive experience of studying in North Vancouver, Canada.

The multi-panel format was an important part of the design strategy, accommodating a significant volume of information while maintaining a compact, practical publication for international recruitment. Consideration was given to how the guide would be handled, unfolded, scanned, and referenced during conversations with prospective students.

The result is a purposeful balance of expressive brand storytelling and functional information design—a tangible recruitment tool that communicates Capilano University's academic opportunities, student community, and sense of place.
