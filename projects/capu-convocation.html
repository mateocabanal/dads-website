Capilano University Convocation Program

Project details

The convocation program serves as a guide to the ceremony and a printed keepsake for graduates. A cover photograph of a graduate embracing someone puts a personal moment at the centre of the publication.

Inside, campus photographs and messages give way to more structured pages for graduate names. An orange page provides a visual pause within the sequence. The editorial challenge was to give ceremonial information a reliable hierarchy while leaving room for photography and the personal significance of the occasion.
