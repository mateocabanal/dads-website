Art + Design

Project details

This self-directed series explores how repeated lines can change the way a familiar symbol reads. An infinity sign, a dollar sign and an angular abstract form share the same striped construction, which makes their differences in contour and movement easier to see.

Directional shadows separate the forms from their backgrounds and make the flat patterns appear dimensional. Shifts in colour and contrast give each image a distinct character while keeping the series visually related.
