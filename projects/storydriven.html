Brand identity

Storydriven Inc.

Project details

A comprehensive brand identity developed to support the evolution of E+S Design into Storydriven Inc., a creative studio focused on storytelling through design.

The rebrand introduced a bold and distinctive visual language, built around a custom wordmark, dimensional monogram, supporting iconography, and a confident red, black, and white colour palette. The identity was designed to communicate the studio's creative ambition while establishing a recognizable and versatile foundation across brand applications.

From the core identity to a thoughtfully produced stationery system, the project brings together expressive typography, strong graphic contrast, and tactile print details to create a cohesive and memorable brand experience.

The creative direction centred on establishing a visual identity that reflected the studio's transition into Storydriven Inc., a name that places narrative, creativity, and purposeful design at the forefront.

Typography became the defining element of the identity. A bold, condensed wordmark establishes a confident presence, while the custom interlocking "SD" monogram introduces a more expressive dimension to the brand. Its sculptural, layered construction creates a sense of depth and movement, giving the identity a distinctive visual signature that can stand independently of the full wordmark.

A restrained palette of vibrant red, black, and white reinforces the brand's personality through contrast, simplicity, and visual impact. The flexibility of the system allows the identity to shift between bold, attention-grabbing expressions and more understated, refined applications without losing consistency.

The stationery extends this thinking into a physical brand experience. Unconventional folded formats, oversized typography, contrasting interior colours, and subtle embossed details transform traditional business cards into memorable expressions of the studio's personality. Careful consideration of materials, finishes, and production techniques adds a tactile quality that reinforces the value of craftsmanship and attention to detail.

The resulting identity balances bold creative expression with a disciplined visual system, establishing a brand that feels confident, contemporary, and unmistakably its own.
