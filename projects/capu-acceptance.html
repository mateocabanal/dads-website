Capilano University Acceptance Package

Project details

Created during remote recruitment, the acceptance package gave admitted students a physical introduction to Capilano University. The box brings together admission documents, a student guide and branded items, turning an administrative milestone into an experience students can open and explore at home.

The exterior establishes the brand through bold colour and type. Inside, campus photography and distinct colour accents give the guide an editorial rhythm, while the letters and certificate retain a clear practical role. The box, guide, stickers and other pieces were designed to read as one set, with each item serving a different purpose once unpacked.
