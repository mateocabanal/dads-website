Brand identity

Bonify

Project details

The Bonify identity was developed for a medical cannabis producer. Its botanical forms express the calming qualities at the centre of the brief. Circular arrangements of leaves create a recognizable motif that can carry the wordmark, repeat as a pattern or simplify into a small icon.

The strain cards give that visual language an editorial function. Colour distinguishes varieties, plant photography provides a visual reference, and a repeatable layout keeps the information easy to compare. On apparel, the circular mark can stand on its own.
