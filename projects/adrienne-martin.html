Brand identity

Adrienne Martin

Project details

The identity for makeup artist Adrienne Martin starts with a simple geometric idea: build a monogram that also reads as a mountain peak. A triangular grid gives the mark its proportions, and overlapping planes create depth within a compact silhouette.

The coral mark brings warmth to the system, while the wordmark and open space give it room on the business card. At that scale, the silhouette has to do most of the work. The spare composition keeps both the name and the symbol easy to read.
