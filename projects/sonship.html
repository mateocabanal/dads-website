Brand identity

Sonship Yachts

Project details

The Sonship Yachts identity draws on maritime iconography through an anchor, rope and an S monogram. The emblem combines these elements into a compact form, while the wordmark uses the anchor as a more restrained reference to the same idea.

The two treatments give the identity options for different placements. The emblem carries the richer nautical detail, and the wordmark provides a simpler signature when the name needs to lead. Silver keeps the focus on line, silhouette and contrast.
